# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to make economic claims about AI agents checkable. That works
only if people feel able to say "this number is wrong" without it becoming
personal. We commit to making participation free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or seniority.

## Expected behaviour

- Attack claims, not people. "This benchmark does not support that conclusion" is
  welcome. "You clearly do not understand statistics" is not.
- Bring evidence. A counterexample, a failing command, or a reproduction is worth
  more than an assertion, and it is the contribution this project values most.
- Accept correction. Being shown wrong in public is the normal cost of publishing
  claims, and it is a service to you.
- Assume competence and good faith in others, and be explicit when you are
  uncertain.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether public or private
- Personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review
- Submitting customer data, secrets, proprietary prompts, or contract pricing,
  which is prohibited on privacy grounds as well as conduct grounds

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, commit
messages, and discussions, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns by opening a GitHub issue, or privately via the contact route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if the matter is sensitive. Reports will be reviewed
and responded to as promptly as the maintainer is able. The maintainer is
obligated to respect the privacy and safety of anyone reporting an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
consequences determined by other project leaders.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.
